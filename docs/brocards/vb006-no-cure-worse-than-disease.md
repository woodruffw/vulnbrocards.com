# VB006: No cure worse than the disease

A vulnerability report can be safely dismissed if the consequences of addressing it
are worse than the consequences of the vulnerability itself.

## Examples

- Most ReDoS "vulnerabilities" require significantly more maintainer effort and attention
  to triage than their threat/risk actually warrants.
